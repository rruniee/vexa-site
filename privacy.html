Vexa privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Vexa is a personal assistant app used only by its creator, on their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What Vexa accesses

When the owner links a Google account, Vexa uses the Gmail API to read emails, apply labels, create drafts, and send emails the owner has approved.

How the data is used

Email content is used only to carry out the owner's own requests, such as summing up new emails or drafting a reply. To understand a request, the text Vexa works with may be processed by the AI model the owner has chosen (Anthropic's Claude, or a model running on the owner's PC).

Storage

Login tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's computer. Vexa has no servers of its own and does not keep copies of emails.

Sharing

Vexa does not sell Google user data, does not share it with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Limited Use

Vexa's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
